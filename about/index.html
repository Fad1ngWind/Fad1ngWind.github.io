PINN-GINav: Physics-Informed GNSS/INS Fusion

Research · First author · 2026

A positioning model that uses IMU motion and continuity between epochs to keep GNSS/INS estimates consistent. Tested on a public urban-driving sequence.

Vehicle-Mounted Ball Balancing

Zhejiang Electronic Design Competition · Problem H · First Prize, 2nd of 452

Tracked a reflective steel ball with a MaixCAM Pro and sent calibrated positions to a TC264. I built the vision pipeline, serial protocol, and control board.

Intelligent Car Competition — 21st

National College Student Intelligent Car Competition · 21st edition, 2026

As the only hardware member of a three-person team, I designed the Loongson carrier board, motor driver, and brushless ESC, then integrated them in the F-model car. It averaged about 2.7 m/s in track tests.
